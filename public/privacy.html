  • Přihlašovací a potvrzovací odkazy ukládáme jako otisk (hash) spolu s e-mailem, účelem, IP adresou požadavku a u registrace či zveřejnění se zadaným jménem a příjmením; mažeme je nejpozději den po vypršení.

  • IP adresy, ze kterých lze zveřejňovat bez účtu, ukládáme se poznámkou správce po dobu, kdy povolení trvá.

Lhůtu 90 dní jsme zvolili, protože oznámení o phishingu, malwaru nebo porušení práv obvykle přicházejí do několika týdnů od zveřejnění a pachatelé často účet smažou, jakmile je obsah nahlášen.

Site details, API keys and passwords

  • For each site we store its name, the account it belongs to, the date it was created and last changed, the number of files and its size.
  • API keys are stored only as a hash, together with their name, first 10 characters (prefix) and the date they were created and last used. Keys (including revoked ones) are kept until the account is deleted and 90 days after that.
  • Sign-in and confirmation links are stored as a hash together with the e-mail, purpose, request IP address and, for registration or publishing, the name entered; they are deleted no later than a day after they expire.
  • Sites published without an account from allowed IP addresses are protected by a password; we store only a hash of it, not the password itself.
  • IP addresses allowed to publish without an account are stored with an administrator's note for as long as the permission lasts.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • When you sign in or confirm publishing a site, we set a sign-in cookie (fp_user), valid for 30 days, by which the service recognises your account. When you choose a language with the CS/EN switch, we keep it in the fp_lang cookie (valid for 1 year) so pages arrive in that language. The service sets no other cookies (uploaded sites may use their own).
  • In your browser's local storage we keep your chosen language. If you tick "Remember my details", we also keep the e-mail and name you entered there so you do not have to type them again; if you untick it, they are deleted the next time you submit. For password-protected sites (published from allowed IP addresses) we keep their names and passwords; the browser sends them to the server only to check that the sites exist and the passwords are valid. This data is not stored on the server, and you delete it by clearing the site data in your browser.

Legal basis: compliance with legal obligations (handling notices under the Digital Services Act and requests under the GDPR) and the controller's legitimate interest in handling the message and defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR).

Deleting your account and retention

  • You can delete your account at any time in its settings and confirm the deletion with a link sent by e-mail. If you cannot sign in, write to martin@freepages.dev. The administrator can also delete accounts and sites; the same periods apply.
  • After an account is deleted, its sites stop being public immediately and the account can no longer sign in. We keep the account details (e-mail, name, IP address at creation), the sites' files and the activity history for 90 more days so we can trace and deal with any abuse of the service that happened before the deletion. We then delete them permanently. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). During this time the data is accessible only to the controller and used solely to investigate reported or detected abuse. We chose 90 days because reports of phishing, malware or infringements usually arrive within a few weeks of publication, and abusers often delete their account once the content is reported.
  • If you want your data deleted immediately, write to martin@freepages.dev. We will delete it without undue delay and within one month at the latest, unless we need it to resolve a specific reported abuse or to protect legal claims, in which case we will tell you.
  • If you create an account with the same e-mail address after deletion, it is a new, empty account; the data of the old account is not linked to it.
  • Server backups (including the database with account details and activity history) are kept for at most 30 days, so deleted data disappears from them no later than 30 days after permanent deletion. Backups are used only to restore the whole service after a failure; any previously deleted data is then deleted again.
